Q+A Katha - Diet 🧠

Question: In the video game DOTA 2, which of these is NOT a hero?

Answer: Dragon Champion - Entertainment: Video Games. Itha kelvi Diet pathi - Answer Dragon Champion. Diet pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Diet pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Diet oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Diet ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Diet la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
Q+A Katha - Sleep 🧠

Question: At the start of a standard game of the Monopoly, if you throw a double six, which square would you land on?

Answer: Electric Company - Entertainment: Board Games. Itha kelvi Sleep pathi - Answer Electric Company. Oru kaalathula oru oor la Sleep nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan Sleep oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan Sleep pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam Sleep ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye Sleep nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, Sleep ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
